About.

A small shelf, kept lean on purpose.

Minecraft Mods is a one-person directory: mods that actually get maintained, listed with a real download link and nothing else in the way — no ad-walled redirect pages, no bundled installers.

Everything you see comes from one small list on the server. Only the site owner can add, edit, or remove a listing; anyone with the link can browse and download.
